## Target Company Analysis: reviews.io

### Website & Positioning

**Positioning:** "Empowering high-growth eCommerce brands to collect, manage, and amplify the voice of their customers through reviews and UGC."

**Target audience:** eCommerce brands (primarily Shopify/BigCommerce), DTC retailers, brands wanting Google Seller Ratings.

**Key value propositions:**
- Approved **Google review partner** — enables star ratings in Google search results (Google Seller Ratings)
- Automated review collection via email, SMS, Klaviyo, Shopify Flow
- 15+ customisable display widgets
- **Reputation Manager** — collect reviews across Google, Facebook, Trustpilot from one dashboard
- Photo, video, and SMS review collection
- Open platform: anyone can review, subject to verification

**Brand voice:** Practical, eCommerce-native, conversion-focused. "Social proof that drives revenue."

### Pricing & Packaging

| Plan | Price/mo | Key Limits |
|------|----------|------------|
| Essentials | ~$29 | 300 email invites/mo; no Google Seller Ratings |
| Start-Up | ~$99 | 300+ orders/mo threshold; Google Seller Ratings included |
| Growth | ~$89 | Advanced collection flows, Klaviyo integration |
| Professional | ~$159 | Deeper product insights |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited invitations |

**Positioning vs. market:** Significantly cheaper entry point than Trustpilot ($99 Starter). Targets Shopify merchants who want Google stars without Trustpilot's price tag.

## Competitive Insights

### Positioning Landscape

| Company | Primary Position | Target Buyer | Price Entry |
|---------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|
| reviews.io | eCommerce social proof + Google Seller Ratings | eCommerce / Shopify brands | $29/mo |
| Trustpilot | Consumer trust & brand credibility | SMB to Enterprise, all verticals | $99/mo |
| Feefo | Verified/fraud-proof reviews | Mid-market retail, automotive, travel | Custom |
| Yotpo | Reviews + Loyalty retention suite | eCommerce, Shopify/BigCommerce | Free |
| Birdeye | Local reputation management | Multi-location, local services | Custom |
| Judge.me | Affordable Shopify-native reviews | Small Shopify merchants | Free |

### Messaging Opportunities for reviews.io

1. **"Trustpilot at half the price"** — The price gap is significant and reviews.io rarely makes this explicit in messaging.
2. **Google Seller Ratings as a standalone hook** — A concrete, measurable outcome (stars in Google search) that most competitors de-emphasise.
3. **Yotpo alternative for brands that don't need loyalty** — Position for brands that want reviews + UGC without paying for a full retention suite.
4. **Video reviews + UGC differentiation** — Strong creative angle given LinkedIn's shift to video ads (video formats up 74% on LinkedIn).

## Recommendations

1. **Run the scraper from an unrestricted network** — `python3 analyse_ads.py` will populate live LinkedIn ad counts for all 6 companies and save screenshots to `CI_reviewsio/`.
2. **Prioritise LinkedIn over Meta for B2B demand gen** — LinkedIn now captures 41% of B2B ad budgets; the eCommerce platform buyer (Marketing Manager / eCommerce Director) is a strong LinkedIn audience.
3. **Lead with Google Seller Ratings in ad creative** — A concrete, search-visible outcome that competitors underplay. "Get stars in Google search" is a high-intent hook.
4. **Test Trustpilot comparison messaging** — reviews.io is 60–70% cheaper than Trustpilot for equivalent features. Competitive "switch from Trustpilot" ads likely perform well.
5. **Monitor Yotpo's free tier strategy** — Yotpo's free plan is capturing small merchants who later upgrade. reviews.io should consider a more compelling free entry point or Shopify-native trial.
